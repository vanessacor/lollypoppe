I'm a frontend engineer at [Wallbox](https://wallbox.com/), a company that makes smart chargers for electric vehicles.

I contribute to frontend development across customer-facing applications and internal tools, mainly using `Vue.js`, `TypeScript`, and `Nuxt`. I build features end to end and maintain our frontend systems, with the goal of keeping the codebase easy to work with as the products grow.

I led the development of the company chatbot frontend. It is integrated across web and native platforms and has around 1,300 monthly users.

I work closely with product managers, designers, and backend engineers to plan and deliver features, often contributing across team and stack boundaries when needed. Over time, my role has grown to include product thinking, such as shaping requirements, refining solutions, and supporting prioritisation alongside product managers.

I built AI-assisted workflows that use LLMs to generate and maintain UI content. They run as a multi-step process, with validation and automated review, so the content stays consistent with our product guidelines.

Beyond feature work, I help improve our development practices through documentation and internal guidelines, and by preparing our codebases for AI-assisted workflows with tools like Claude Code.
