From 2021 to 2025, I was a software engineer at [Glovo](https://about.glovoapp.com/), a food delivery service. I worked as a frontend engineer on the Partners app and web app, the tools partners used to manage their stores inside Glovo: their menus, their opening hours, and their own metrics, such as order details, order volume, and reviews. I developed advanced features for this large-scale application, taking it from inception to a mature state that served a diverse user base every month.

I refactored legacy code, introduced modular design patterns, improved test coverage, and shipped features on the most recent version of `Vue.js`.

As a team member, I collaborated closely with backend developers, UX/UI designers, and product managers to align on project requirements and deliverables.

Additionally, I led initiatives to write and update documentation for features, APIs, and workflows, so the team had clear, shared knowledge.
